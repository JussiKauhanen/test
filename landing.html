No NearChat server receives the content.

NearChat’s current architecture creates the transfer on one phone and reconstructs it on the other. There is no NearChat account or message inbox in the middle.

Prototype note: direct transfer is not the same as encryption. Do not use the current prototype for passwords or highly sensitive material until encrypted payloads and peer verification are added.
